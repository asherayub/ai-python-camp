5. **Act as a professional doctor and explain to me briefly what are the early signs of diabetes in simple terms**

Early signs of Diabetes Mellitus often start slowly and many people ignore them. Here are the most common early symptoms explained simply:

### 1\. Frequent urination 🚽

You may feel the need to urinate more often than usual, especially at night. This happens because extra sugar in the blood makes the kidneys produce more urine.

### 2\. Feeling very thirsty 🥤

Because you are losing more water through urine, your body becomes dehydrated, making you feel very thirsty most of the time.

### 3\. Increased hunger

Even after eating, you might feel hungry again quickly. Your body isn’t using sugar (glucose) properly for energy.

### 4\. Unusual tiredness

You may feel weak or very tired because your cells are not getting enough energy from sugar.

### 5\. Blurred vision

High sugar levels can cause temporary swelling in the eyes, making your vision blurry.

### 6\. Slow healing wounds

Cuts, infections, or wounds may take longer to heal than usual.

### 7\. Frequent infections

People may get skin infections, gum problems, or urinary infections more often.

### 8\. Tingling or numbness in hands and feet

Some people feel pins and needles or numbness, especially in the feet.

Here’s a detailed analysis and action plan for Brew Haven Café based strictly on the information you provided:

## 1\. Situation Analysis

* Business Size & Resources: Small café with 5 employees and a modest revenue of \~$120,000/year. Limited staff and budget constrain growth options.  
* Market Position: Competing with nearby cafés and chains. Currently has low brand awareness and stagnant sales.

Summary: The café has an appealing niche (specialty drinks and pastries) and a loyal small customer base but is constrained by low awareness, competition, limited staff, and budget. Immediate gains can be made through low-cost, high-impact marketing and customer experience improvements.

---

## 2\. Key Problems

1. Low brand awareness in the local community.  
2. Declining foot traffic over the past 6 months.  
3. Stagnant sales, especially of specialty items.  
4. Limited digital engagement despite having social media accounts.  
5. No delivery or online ordering, missing potential convenience-driven sales.  
6. Resource constraints: small team, small marketing budget, limited space.

---

## 3\. Step-by-Step Action Plan (Prioritized)

### Step 1: Boost Local Visibility and Foot Traffic (High Priority)

Actions:

* Launch targeted local promotions: “Happy Hour” specialty drinks, student discounts, or breakfast combo deals.  
* Partner with local offices and student groups for bulk orders or loyalty programs.  
* Organize small in-store events (e.g., coffee tasting or pastry sampling) to attract new customers.

Why Important:  
Foot traffic is declining, and brand awareness is low. Low-cost, local promotions directly drive more people into the café and increase potential sales, leveraging existing resources.

### Step 2: Improve Social Media Engagement (High Priority)

Actions:

* Post daily content highlighting specialty drinks, pastries, and behind-the-scenes barista skills.  
* Run Instagram/Facebook contests (“Tag a friend for a free pastry”) to increase shares and engagement.  
* Use geo-targeted ads (\~$5–10/day) to reach local residents within walking/driving distance.

Why Important:  
Social media is underutilized but essential for reaching the target audience (students and office workers) and promoting specials. Increased engagement can translate into higher foot traffic.

### Step 3: Promote Specialty Items and Upsell (Medium Priority)

Actions:

* Train baristas to upsell specialty drinks and pastries during ordering.  
* Introduce limited-time specialty drinks to create urgency.  
* Display menu highlights and combo options near the counter and on social media.

Why Important:  
Boosting sales of high-margin items increases revenue without increasing foot traffic. Limited-time offers encourage repeat visits.

### Step 4: Introduce a Simple Online Ordering Option (Medium-Low Priority)

Actions:

* Start with phone-in orders for pick-up, promoted via social media and in-store signage.  
* If budget allows, explore basic online ordering apps that are cost-effective.

Why Important:  
Convenience can attract students and office workers who may not have time to sit in. Even a minimal online ordering setup can increase sales without expanding space.

### Step 5: Leverage Loyal Customers for Word-of-Mouth Marketing (Medium-Low Priority)

Actions:

* Implement a referral program: customers get a free drink/pastry for bringing friends.  
* Collect customer emails/social media handles for announcements and promotions.

Why Important:  
Loyal customers are your most effective marketing channel. Encouraging them to bring friends expands reach without extra cost.

## 4\. Prioritization

1. Step 1: Boost local visibility/foot traffic  
2. Step 2: Improve social media engagement  
3. Step 3: Promote specialty items and upsell  
4. Step 4: Introduce online ordering/pick-up  
5. Step 5: Leverage loyal customers for referrals

Rationale: Steps 1–2 are immediate drivers of foot traffic and awareness. Steps 3–5 enhance revenue and long-term growth, building on increased foot traffic and engagement.

## 5\. Key Takeaways

* Focus on low-cost, high-impact marketing: local promotions, events, and social media.  
* Foot traffic is the main lever—all other actions (social media, upselling, referrals) should support this.  
* Use the café’s unique offerings (specialty drinks, pastries) as a differentiator against chains.  
* Maximize the existing small team and loyal customer base instead of trying to expand space or budget.
